Introduction

Welcome to . We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data in our application. JIC Ture does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing occurs locally on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

For the clarity of our users, we explicitly state that we do not collect or upload the following information:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (Name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location Data
  • Contacts or Address Book
  • Device Identifiers used for tracking across apps
  • Health or Financial Information

Local Data Storage

Any data generated by using the app (such as game scores, settings, or cached media) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage. This data is:

  • Under your sole control.
  • Not synchronized with our servers.
  • Permanently deleted when you uninstall the application.

Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media sharing services that would require data transmission.

Data Security

Since no data leaves your device, our primary security measure is standard local file encryption provided by the Android OS. We strongly recommend keeping your device screen lock enabled to prevent unauthorized access to your local data.

Children's Privacy

Our application is not designed for users under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal information, we are compliant with COPPA regulations by default.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in our practices. All updates will be effective upon posting to this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the policy.
